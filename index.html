This ESC is running factory firmware — the tool is read-only for it.
Bytes 0x05–0x10 on this layout are the ESC's own name string, not settings, and the Rockwolf fields (Crawl Torque, brushed mode) do not exist in this firmware. Nothing is wrong with your ESC.

While this ESC is on factory firmware: its low-voltage cutoff reads the battery on the wrong input for this board. On this ESC it is switched off — and switching it on will not make it read the pack correctly either. The Rockwolf firmware fixes that along with everything else.

You can still tune this ESC normally with the config tool it already works with (Config Tool 1.82 for brushed, 1.93 for brushless) — the only thing this web tool cannot do on this firmware is write.

Crawl Tuning

Motor
Which kind of motor is plugged in. Switches the whole drive engine — no reflash needed.
Match the number on your motor's label. Keeps the speed math honest.
From your motor's spec sheet (12 or 14 for most micro outrunners). Wrong poles = wrong speeds.
Forward/Reverse: center stick = stopped, push/pull to drive — required for crawler radios. Reverse rotation: flip if your rig drives backwards.
Crawl Drive
How much of the trigger is smooth crawl drive. Past this point it switches to normal high-speed mode. 100% = never switches.
Raw strength ceiling of the crawl drive. With Crawl Torque set, leave at 10 and let the torque setting do the managing.
How hard the motor pulls, in real amps — held steady through binds, speed changes and battery sag. In brushed mode this drives the torque assist. 0 = unregulated.
Full-throttle current allowance as a multiple of Crawl Torque. Low = tame sprint zone; high = big top-end headroom.
Extra kick to break the motor loose from a dead stop. In brushed mode this powers the kickstart pulse.
Smallest power ever sent to a turning motor. Nudge up if there's a dead zone right off idle.
How fast power is allowed to rise when you squeeze. Lower = softer, more progressive trigger. Works in both motor modes.
The smooth low-speed drive itself — the heart of the Rockwolf feel. Leave on.
Stall Recovery
How much extra push gets added when the motor starts bogging down. 0 = off.
How quickly that extra push arrives when a wheel bites. High values can lurch when it breaks free.
Master switch for the boost system above.
Braking
How hard the motor grips once the rig has stopped. 10 = locked on hills.
Engine-brake feel while moving. Higher = more hold-back when you let off; lower = freer coasting.
Master switch for drag brake when the trigger is released.
Battery
Cuts motor power when the battery runs low (steering stays alive). Trips after ~10s below the threshold; power cycle to reset.
Per-cell trip voltage — cell count is detected automatically. 3.0V is the classic LiPo floor; 3.3V leaves a safety margin.
Whole-pack trip voltage. For 5S–6S use this mode (not per-cell) — e.g. 18.0V for a 6S floor. 19V is the sensor's ceiling.
Advanced
Fires the motor slightly early for extra top-end speed. Costs heat and low-speed smoothness — crawlers should leave this at 0°.
Motor speed at the moment it exits sine mode into high-speed mode. + hands off hotter, − hands off gentler. 0 = stock.
unsaved changes
Protocol is locked to Servo (crawler receivers). Values write to the ESC and take effect after the automatic reboot.
